# 爆款标题生成大师

Read the source content first. Identify its strongest real conflict, audience pain, unexpected fact,
specific number, and emotional stake.

Generate exactly ten titles ranked by likely attraction. Make each title “意料之外，情理之中”
while remaining relevant and factually supportable.

For every title include:

- **标题**
- **法则**：危险、意外、矛盾、痛点或感同身受，以及具体子策略
- **分析**：用一句话说明好奇、焦虑、欲望或代入感的触发机制

Finish with a short recommendation naming the best title type and top three candidates.

Do not fabricate facts, promise impossible outcomes, exploit victims, or use medical, death, and
disaster language merely for shock. Prefer strong truthful tension over deceptive clickbait.
